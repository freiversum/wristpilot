# Privacy policy – Wristpilot

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

Wristpilot is a Connect IQ app for Garmin watches and Edge bike computers, published by Christoph
Langner (contact: https://linuxundich.de/en/contact/). This policy covers the watch app and the free Wristpilot Bridge (the Windows
program and the bridge built into Tretlager).

## What the watch app processes

- **Button presses and commands** you trigger on the watch (for example "turn left"). They are
  sent over Bluetooth Low Energy to the Wristpilot Bridge on your own computer, and nowhere else.
- **Settings** you change in the app (vibration, start page). They stay on the watch.

The watch app does not use the Garmin Connect app or the internet, and does not read position,
heart rate, activity or other sensor data.

## What the bridge processes

The bridge runs on your computer. It turns commands into keyboard input for Zwift and, if you
turn it on, offers them to trainer apps on your local network (OpenBikeControl). It keeps its
settings and a log file on your computer. It does not send any data to the developer.

Only if you switch on the optional **Zwift Companion link**, the bridge uses your Zwift sign-in
to register your computer with Zwift as the "phone" of the Zwift Companion app, and Zwift's game
then talks to the bridge over your local network. Your Zwift token is stored on your computer only
(Windows: encrypted with your Windows account; Linux/Tretlager: in your keyring). Wristpilot never
sees your Zwift password. This data goes to Zwift, Inc. under Zwift's own privacy policy; the
developer receives none of it.

## Data sent to the developer or to Garmin

None. Wristpilot has no analytics, no crash reporting of its own and no servers. Any data you
submit through the app is submitted to the developer, not to Garmin, and Garmin has no
responsibility or liability for it.
